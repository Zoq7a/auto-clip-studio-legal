Windows desktop video tool

Auto Clip Studio

Turn long-form videos and permitted YouTube recordings into shorter, captioned vertical videos. Review clips and choose whether to send them to connected publishing accounts.

How it works

Videos are downloaded or imported and processed on your own Windows PC. Speech recognition generates captions, then the app exports vertical clips. Publishing uses your authorization through YouTube or TikTok when you choose to connect.
