Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Auto Clip Studio (the “App”), a locally run Windows Python application, handles information when you use its video clipping and optional publishing tools.

1. Who operates the app

Operator and privacy contact: Majdi Yassin — majdi.school.work@gmail.com. This public website explains the App; it does not provide user sign-in or receive video uploads.

2. Information processed

The App processes video files you choose to import, YouTube links you provide, video audio, generated transcripts and subtitles, edited short clips, titles, descriptions, hashtags, and publishing history. These files may contain personal information appearing in the footage.

3. Local storage

The App saves processing files and generated outputs in local project folders (including its runs directory). It stores linked-account credentials and OAuth access/refresh tokens in the operating system’s credential store using Python keyring, rather than writing those values into the website. Publishing history and queued jobs are stored locally. If you enable the Storage Saver option, the App may delete the downloaded full-length source after output checks succeed. Generated clips and history are not automatically removed by this option.

4. External services

When you import content from YouTube, the App requests video metadata/media through YouTube and its supporting download tool. When you authorize uploading, selected videos, titles, descriptions, and related metadata are transmitted to YouTube/Google or TikTok using their APIs. OAuth account authorization occurs through the relevant provider; this App does not ask for your account password. YouTube and TikTok process transferred data under their own privacy policies.

Optional speech-recognition models may be downloaded from external model-hosting services when first used; actual transcription runs on your machine under the current App setup.

5. Why the App processes information

To clip source videos, generate and display subtitles, prepare exports, publish content when you ask it to, remember publishing outcomes, and avoid accidental duplicate uploads.

6. Sharing and selling

The App does not intentionally sell personal data or operate a separate cloud database for video content. Data you choose to publish is transferred to the connected provider. GitHub may collect standard website access data when hosting these pages.

7. Retention and your choices

Local recordings, clips, transcripts, publishing history, and queued jobs remain on your computer until you delete them or use an available cleanup option. You can revoke app access from your Google or TikTok account security settings. Revoking access may stop future uploads but does not delete previously published videos. For complete disconnection, also clear locally stored application credentials and tokens.

8. Security

The App relies on the security of your Windows account and local credential manager for stored tokens. Keep your computer, credentials, and exported videos secure. No software or network transmission can be guaranteed absolutely secure.

9. Children and sensitive material

The App is intended for people who are permitted to manage the videos and accounts they use. Do not upload footage that you lack the right or consent to publish, or sensitive personal information without an appropriate lawful basis.

10. Policy updates and contact

This policy may be updated when the App’s processing changes. Questions, access requests, and deletion concerns: majdi.school.work@gmail.com.
